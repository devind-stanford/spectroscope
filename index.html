Automatic

Point the spectroscope at the light and put the band on the whole spectrum, violet to red. Auto-calibrate then finds the known lines and lists them below, where they can be checked, removed or added to.

By hand

  1. Tap the plot on a line you recognise. Use the arrows under the plot for single pixels.
  2. Pick a known line, or type any wavelength, then add it.
  3. Repeat for as many lines as you like. Two give a straight fit and three a curved one; every line after that averages out small errors and shows how well the lines agree.

Keep stores the typed wavelength under My lines in the list above, so it is there next time.

Shift to match slides the whole calibration sideways so the cursor reads the wavelength above. Use it after remounting the phone, with one line you trust.

Marked lines

    Spectrum is a CSV table plus a small settings file. Plot is a PNG. Image is the frame as analysed. On iPhone the share sheet opens: choose Save to Files, AirDrop or another app. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose it there.

    Privacy

    This page works entirely on your device. Camera frames, spectra and calibrations are never uploaded: the page has no server, no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Calibrations and settings are kept in this browser's storage.

    Limits

    A phone camera adjusts exposure and colour by itself and applies tone curves, so heights are relative and approximate. Positions of lines are reliable once calibrated. Each camera, video size and orientation keeps its own calibration.
